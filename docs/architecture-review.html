1 · Stamp kind at the ask seam

Strong ports & adapters accepted, revised

daemon.py:120-147, 1328-1335 · ipc/protocol.py · hook.py:236 · the three TS shims

Before — inferred from shape

  H->>I: kind "question", no tool

After — enforced at one seam

sequenceDiagram
  participant H as hook.py
  participant V as the three TS shims
  participant I as _invoke
  Note over I: absent kind → infer and stamp
unknown explicit kind → ProtocolError
  H->>I: kind "question"
  V->>I: kind "question" | "approval"
  I->>I: accepted or refused

Problem. Approval-versus-question is load-bearing three times per ask (auto-decide, routing, escalation re-derivation) and three of four shims classify by the absence of tool. The contract is written in prose at daemon.py:128-133 — shims must send kind explicitly — but _invoke never enforces it, and ipc/protocol.py never mentions it.

Solution. Validate in _invoke: an unknown explicit value is a protocol error; an absent value is inferred and stamped.

Constraint from the adversarial pass: do not fail closed on absence. Shims are staged at spawn, so a container outliving a daemon upgrade runs an old shim — refusing it would break in-flight asks. That is an availability regression, not deny-by-default.
  • Prose contract becomes enforced
  • Shims stop knowing the heuristic
  • Reinforces ADR 0001, does not re-litigate it
  • Precondition for candidate 2's interface

3 · Publish bind_ipc and split hook verdicts from transport

Worth exploring local-substitutable two claims struck

daemon.py:1085 · guest/hook.py:208-312 · tests/test_daemon.py — 35 call sites

Problem. Tests reach daemon._serve_container 35 times because there is no public "registered but not running" seam, and hook.py main() mixes transport with the four-way verdict mapping.

Solution. Publish bind_ipc(container) (a rename), and split decide(event, policy, ask) → verdict from the socket/stdio edges.

Struck: "health-check reuses it" — no health check exists in the codebase. Struck: "decision matrix only testable in a sandbox" — the bun conformance suite already covers the pi / opencode2 / v1 matrices off-sandbox; for hook.py it is convention, not structure (it is stdlib-only and host-runnable today).
  • The interface is the test surface
  • 35 private reaches become one call
  • Verdict mapping testable without bwrap

7 · One compose loader (perf, not correctness)

Worth exploring correctness claim withdrawn

teams.py:42-65 · web/app.py:68-91, 10 endpoints · doctor.py:40-64 · compose.py:497

Problem. Three loaders, three REPO_ROOT/COMPOSE_PATH copies; compose.py shells npm root -g at import, and the web console re-execs it across ten endpoints.

Solution. Share COMPOSE_PATH and memoize the npm lookup (cache keyed on roles-dir mtime). No new seam, no lifetime unification.

Withdrawn: the "doctor reports a different role world" claim was the report's only asserted live bug, and it is false — doctor reads compose only for AGENT_SETUP (doctor.py:47, 149, 202, 533) and never ROLES, so custom roles cannot affect it. The differing lifetimes are also deliberate: teams/web need fresh loads to see just-saved roles; doctor is a one-shot CLI over a static table.

9 · RoleRegistry — rejected as scoped

Rejected kept to prevent re-suggestion

roles.py:273-325 · examples/roles-and-personas/compose.py:810, 824

Why it was suggested: the custom-role registry lives as mutated state on a synthesised compose module, with identity-based refresh.

Why it is rejected: the invariant it claims is missing is enforced everywhere roles are consumed — merge refuses shadowing (roles.py:309-311), save refuses to create it (roles.py:288), delete refuses built-ins (roles.py:273-275), and both role-consuming loaders install custom roles (teams.py:64, web/app.py:89). "Doctor does not enforce it" was vacuous: doctor consumes no roles. More decisively, the mutation is forced: compose() is a standalone example CLI reading its own module-global ROLES, so a registry "passed to validate and spawn" cannot reach spawn without rewriting the example.

Reopen only as part of a larger decision to move ROLES ownership out of examples/.

Top recommendation

1 · Stamp kind at the ask seam

Smallest change, and it unlocks the largest: candidate 2's extracted module should take a declared kind at its ask() rather than inheriting a heuristic. It reinforces ADR 0001 instead of re-litigating it, needs one validation point in _invoke plus one line per shim, and must infer-on-absence so upgraded daemons keep serving containers running older staged shims.

Then candidate 2 — approvals out of daemon.py, with an honest ~7-method interface and an explicit decision on where request_capability and escalate land.

Correction to the original top pick: the "most urgent bug" footnote — "doctor reports a role world that does not match the one you spawn into" — has been removed. It was false.
